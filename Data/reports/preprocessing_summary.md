Origin of the data: products, suppliers, customers, prices, costs, lead times and all sales are the real source records. Purchase orders, deliveries and stock levels are simulated from the real sales (see `simulation_report.md`).

Derived values and their rules:

- `avg_daily_demand`, `std_daily_demand`: units ordered per day over the last 365 days, counting days without sales as zero.
- `safety_stock`, `reorder_point`, `order_up_to_level`: the replenishment policy in force on each day, as set by the simulation from the preceding 91 days of demand.
- `days_of_cover`: stock on hand divided by average daily demand over the last 28 days.
- `is_stockout` / `stockout_days`: days that ended with no stock while customers were waiting.
- `demand_trend_pct`: units in the last 28 days against the 28 days before.
- `abc_class`: products ranked by revenue; A = first 80% of revenue, B = next 15%, C = the rest.
- `erp/sales_orders.csv` has one row per sales order line. `status` follows the simulated stock: Pending while the line waits for stock, Processing once shipped but not yet delivered (shipped date plus the order's own delivery time is after the last day), otherwise Completed. The source's own status is in `cleaned/sales_orders.csv`.
- `erp/purchase_orders.csv` has one row per purchase-order line, quantity in units (outers x quantity_per_outer); status is Delivered when the line was received, otherwise Ordered.
- `erp/suppliers.csv`: `avg_delivery_days` and `delivery_reliability` (on-time %) are measured on the simulated deliveries. Only the 7 suppliers that supply products are included; the 6 service providers in the source are left out.
- `erp/products.csv` `sku` is derived from the product id; the source has no SKU.
