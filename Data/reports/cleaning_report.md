## What was changed

- Kept 10 of the 29 source tables' worth of content; lookup tables were folded in as names.
- Dropped: `last_edited_by` / `last_edited_when` audit columns, `photo`, `custom_fields`, `search_details`, `returned_delivery_data`, bank account details, fax numbers, postal (PO box) addresses, and columns that are entirely empty in the source (`comments`, `internal_comments`, `delivery_instructions`, `delivery_run`, `run_position`, `credit_note_reason`).
- Dropped `is_undersupply_backordered` (the same value on all 73,595 orders).
- Whitespace normalised in names (9 product and customer names had double or trailing spaces).
- `t`/`f` flags became `True`/`False`; dates are ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`; identifiers and quantities are integers.
- Added, computed directly from source columns: `line_total_excl_tax` (quantity x unit_price), `ordered_units` / `received_units` (outers x quantity_per_outer), `expected_cost_per_unit` (price per outer / quantity_per_outer), `primary_category`, `fulfilment_status`.
- No rows were removed: there were no duplicate rows, duplicate keys or orphaned references.

## Problems in the source data (reported, not repaired)

These come from the way the sample database was generated. They affect the inventory and procurement side; the sales side is consistent.

1. **Stock on hand does not follow from the stock movements.** `quantity_on_hand` equals the sum of movements for 0 of 227 products, and the running balance of movements goes negative at some point for 227 products.
2. **Stock levels are far above demand.** Median stock on hand is 76,373 units against a median demand of 4.9 units a day, i.e. a median of 16 years of cover. Only 2 products are below their reorder level.
3. **Purchasing is concentrated on few products.** 0 products have no stock receipt at all and 218 received fewer units than were sold, while total receipts (139,418,326 units) are 15.6 times total sales (8,950,628 units).
4. **Deliveries are never late.** Every received purchase-order line arrived 1 to 3 days after ordering (mean 1.44); 0 lines arrived after the expected date, which is usually 20 days out. Supplier delay analysis has nothing to find here.
5. **Few active suppliers.** 7 of 13 suppliers supply any product, and 2 of them account for 2,040 of 2,074 purchase orders.
6. **Old orders left open.** 3,085 sales orders (4.2%) were never picked or invoiced, dating back to 2013-01-12.
7. **Cost at or above selling price** for 4 products (`last_cost_price` >= `unit_price`).
